## 2025-02-24 - Accessibility for Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons without accessible names (like `aria-label` or visible text) pose a significant challenge for screen reader users and those navigating by keyboard. In this app's specific interactive components (like floating buttons and toggle controls), a lack of explicit labels made primary actions like "Toggle Music", "Open Message", and social links indecipherable to assistive technology. Hover tooltips (using the `title` attribute) also proved to be a simple but necessary enhancement for visual users to understand the purpose of ambiguous icons before interacting.
**Action:** When implementing icon-only controls (like `lucide-react` icons inside raw buttons or links), always include an `aria-label` for assistive technologies and a `title` attribute to provide a native browser tooltip for visual hover states.
